Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026.

Lunar Travel respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and the choices you have. It is written with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) in mind.

Who we are

Lunar Travel provides private road travel in Gauteng, the Free State and the Northern Cape. You can reach us at hello@lunartravel.co.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in an enquiry: your name, email address, phone number and the trip details you write.
  • Basic technical information your browser shares with any website, such as your IP address and device type, when you visit this site.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and plan your trip.
  • To keep the site secure and working.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

Only the Lunar Travel team and the service providers we use to run the site and send email, who may only use it for that purpose. We may share information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond and to deal with any booking that follows, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Email hello@lunartravel.co and we will respond. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Cookies

See our cookie policy.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
